Handles stay linked by default. Hold Alt to move one, Shift for 45°, or press ⇧C to create an equal pair. Arrow keys nudge anchors; hold Shift for 10 units or Alt for 0.1. Alignment guides snap anchors. Scroll to pan; Ctrl/⌘-scroll zooms. Select exactly what you want to remove, then press Delete. Use Ctrl/⌘Z to undo.
